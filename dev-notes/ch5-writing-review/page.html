The guide, in short

The guide teaches that a bad text is usually not wrong, it makes the reader work, so the order is fixed before the style. Its eleven aspects, which each card cites by number:

  1. Orient the reader first. Orientação inicial
  2. State the main proposition early. Proposição adiada
  3. Keep related information close. Associação das hipóteses
  4. One stable name per concept. Coerência terminológica
  5. Define before use, and say how it is measured. Definição dos conceitos
  6. Recall details retrieved from far away. Recuperação de informação
  7. Progress argumentatively. Ordem / progressão do argumento
  8. Vary rhythm by function, not terminology. Ritmo
  9. Match the strength of claims to the evidence. Âmbito das conclusões
  10. Close a subject by its role. Fecho do assunto
  11. Be concise without losing precision. Concisão sem perda de precisão
