ORI-02aCh 2 §2.1 Thrust (l.141)QuickOpen

The simpler engine models are described only in Appendix A.4

Writing guide

  • Principle 1
    Orient the reader first
  • Principle 3
    Keep related information close

What it refers to

Chapter 2 ends its treatment of the first-stage thrust with “The simulator also offers a simpler model, with thrust and specific impulse held at their sea-level values.” Chapter 5 Table cap_rocket lists four such models (sea-level, vacuum, mean value, and a ramp with flight time).

Reading

Chapter 5's Tables cap_rocket and cap_guidance list options that no chapter describes. Your chapter-role rule keeps implementation options out of the theory chapters, so the text below is written as a modeling choice, with no mention of the simulator. The existing sentence names the simulator in a theory chapter and covers one of the four models. The proposal replaces it with the four models and the one condition that matters (thrust and specific impulse must follow the same model). Its content comes from Appendix A.4.

Recommendation

Replace the sentence. The alternative is to leave Chapter 2 as it is and describe the four models in Chapter 5 §5.1; Chapter 2 is shorter then, but the reader meets the models in the implementation chapter first.
