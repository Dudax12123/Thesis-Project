- **PMP Stage-2 frame.** The rotation-free Stage-2 equations used to propagate the ground-relative
  state against `√(μ/r) − v_rot`; in those equations that target is the apoapsis of an ellipse with
  periapsis −890 km, and a refinement deleted the circularisation burn to reach it.
  `INDIRECT_PMP_STAGE2_FRAME = "inertial"` (default) converts the hand-off with an exact planar
  transform and targets `√(μ/r)`. Outputs stay ground-relative. `"rotating"` reproduces old runs.

  ignition) cannot be satisfied. The stationarity conditions of the solver's own durations, written

  bound), `H_burn1_end = H_last_burn_start`, `H_burn_end < 0` — finite-difference verified to 1e-7.
  `INDIRECT_PMP_TRANSVERSALITY = "duration_stationarity"` (default); `"pontani_eq38"` reproduces the
  old objective bit-identically.
- Solver also returns `H_last_burn_start`, `H_burn1_end`, `costates_final`, `state_final_propagated`.
