## 3. Flags — things the thesis must state or the next session must know

1. **The gravity turn beats PEG by 978 kg and the PMP benchmark by 172 kg** at equal budget. The
   documented arc-1 targeting limitation is the prime suspect: under the coast architecture every
   closed-loop law steers the pre-coast burn as a direct insertion and the swarm's coast then
   discards that plan (comment above Arc 1 in `run_pso_coast_trajectory`). It penalises PEG and not
   the passive law. **The PMP is therefore not an upper bound at this budget** — the same pattern as
   2026-09-11.
2. **The insertion target is not a circular orbit.** With the kept unprojected credit, every
   rotation-on run (laws and PMP alike, before and after 7d) inserts at 500 km with zero vertical
   speed but ~125 m/s below circular: the apoapsis of a 55–74 km × 500 km ellipse, e ≈ 0.033. The
   archive's eccentricity column cannot show it — the target subtracts the same 440.8 m/s the orbit
   conversion adds back, so it reads ~0 **by construction**. The two rotation-off cases are genuinely
   circular. Ch. 6 must disclose this.
3. **That target is reachable ballistically**, so an optimiser can skip the circularisation burn
   (~123 m/s, ≈ 1 t). The polish did exactly that (+1.5–1.7 t, coast ~1 300 s, last burn < 1 s) and the
   laws show it in milder form (`show_exp_shooting` 5 s last burn after a 506 s coast; `gt_baseline`
   22 s after 409 s). The 2000 s bound widens the exposure. Part of any PMP margin over the laws is
   this, not guidance quality.
4. **`peg_direct` is 10 km short** (489.73 km): a valid run of a bad architecture, J′ = 3.06 dominated
   by the altitude penalty. **`gt_direct` is suborbital by design** (periapsis −1 115 km) — its
   propellant figure is not comparable.
5. **`gt_apogee` excludes its circularisation burn** from the integrated trajectory, which is why its
   losses are ~600 m/s below everything else. Not comparable without adding `circularisation_dv`.
6. **Propellant and J′ do not rank the same way** (J′ carries the penalties): `pmp_vacuum` seed 3 has
   the best J′ but 179 kg less propellant than seed 42. State which metric Ch. 6 reports.
7. **A polish would need its own bibliography.** Verified support exists for the method if ever
   wanted: `pontani2010pso` (PSO choosing initial costates), `BettsSurvey1998`, `RaoPseudo`,
   `hull2008initial`, `nocedal2006numerical`, `pontani2013ascent`; to add would be Trélat 2012 (JOTA
   154(3):713–758, shooting + continuation) and Moré 1978 (LNM 630:105–116, the LM algorithm SciPy's
   `lm` implements). **The duration-stationarity conditions themselves are our own derivation**
   (finite-difference verified), replacing Pontani's Eq. 38 — no citation covers them.

## 6. Traps learned this session

- **`--out` resolves against `Tese/src`.** A repo-root-relative path writes to
  `Tese/src/Tese/src/Output/...`. Pass an absolute path. (Cost one re-file of ten seed runs.)
- **`--case` writes no CSV.** Running cases as parallel `--case` processes is the way to use the
  machine, but the batch summary must then be assembled with `run_results_matrix._rows_for_csv` +
  `_write_csv`.
- **A `pso_coast` `.npz` repeats the SECO sample already converted to inertial speed** (7612.68 vs
  7171.91). Read the insertion state from the row or the sample *before* it, never
  `searchsorted(t_seco, "right") - 1`. This produced a wrong "apoapsis 1 780 km" in one analysis.
- **The PMP polish script's stale starts**: `dev-notes/pmp_local_refine.py` / `pmp_swarm_polish.py`
  JSONs from 2026-09-13 are pre-Stage-1-pseudo-forces and pre-7d; they are opt-in
  (`--old-refine-starts`) for that reason.
- **The config's own `TGO_ESTIMATOR` is `peg_new`**, but the matrix baseline overrides it to
  `rocket_equation` for every case. Only matters outside the harness.
- Solver console printouts are **rounded**; the archived `decision_vector` is not. A vector copied
  from the console missed the archived insertion by 107 m / 0.14 m/s.
