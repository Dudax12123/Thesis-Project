  `--only gt_,peg_,show_` is exactly the 18 non-PMP cases.
- **`pso_tail_improvement_frac` is span-normalised** and reads 0.0000 for everything when the
  objective starts high (the PMP starts at J = 149.9). Use absolute end-of-run improvement
  from the `pso_gen`/`pso_gbest` arrays instead.
- **No law steers in Stage 1** in any of the 20 cases — max |α| before MECO is 0.0000° for all

## C. Transversality — the arithmetic, confirmed

`w_J = 1.0` and `J_nd = (t_f − t_cf)/T_MAX_2`, so **one unit of J′ is exactly `T_MAX_2 · mdot_2`
= 338.72 s × 273.589 kg/s = 92670.0 kg**, the Stage-2 tank. Therefore:

| case | `obj_transv` | % of J′ | in propellant | H residual |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| `pmp_baseline` | 0.015955 | 2.01 % | **1478.6 kg** | 11.44 (velocity units) |
| `pmp_vacuum` | 0.024019 | 3.03 % | **2225.9 kg** | 17.23 |

`obj_alt`, `obj_vel` and `obj_fpa` are all ~1e-6 — six orders of magnitude smaller. Transversality
is the *only* term competing with burn time; insertion is essentially exact.

Two things follow, and they pull opposite ways:

1. **The 1478.6 kg is an upper bound, not a predicted gain.** It is what the penalty adds to J′ at
   the optimum found. What removing it actually recovers is whatever the swarm can convert, which
   is what the A/B below measures.
2. **`pmp_vacuum` has the LARGER residual**, which argues against the obvious explanation. If the
   residual were model mismatch — costates derived on drag-free EOM, trajectory flown with drag and
   pressure-dependent thrust — then `pmp_vacuum`, whose dynamics are much closer to what the
   costates assume, should sit closer to H = 0. It does not; it is 50 % worse. That points at
   under-convergence rather than mismatch, and means the penalty may be buying less optimality
   than it costs.

The condition being enforced is H = 0 (free final time). Note it partly duplicates work the
objective already does: `J_nd` minimises burn time directly, and H = 0 *is* the stationarity
condition for burn time. At a true extremal both are satisfied at once and the penalty is free.
It costing 2–3 % is itself evidence the swarm is not reaching one.

The `w = 0` arm is 7.5× worse on its own preferred metric. The transversality term is not merely a

region of costate space where the trajectory actually closes on the target orbit.

**And the cost is mostly a convergence artifact.** `obj_transv` against evaluation count:

| evals | `obj_transv` | in propellant | insertion penalty sum |
|---|---|---|---|
| 125 000 | 0.063092 | 5846 kg | 1.76e−02 |

Doubling the budget cut the residual 3.95× and the insertion penalties by 5400×. The 1478.6 kg is
what the term costs *at the budget flown*, not a structural handicap; it shrinks as the swarm
approaches an extremal, which is exactly what theory says should happen (at a true extremal H = 0
holds and the penalty is free). A third point at 250×1000 post-fix is running into

**Recommendation.** Keep `PENALTY_W_TRANSVERS = 10.0`. The fairness concern is real but the remedy
is not the weight — it is budget, plus saying so in the text. Chapter 6 should state that the PMP

**Caveat.** One seed, one budget, and both arms are under-converged relative to the authoritative

result is unambiguous — an orbit that re-enters is not a marginal call — but the magnitudes are
