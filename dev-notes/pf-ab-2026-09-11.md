## Reading it

**The per-law differences are not a force-model effect that can be read off this A/B.** They
change sign across laws, span −7.6 t to +7.4 t, and in every pair move with the steering loss —
a property of the trajectory the swarm settled on — rather than with the pseudo-force exposure,
which is essentially the same for every law:

| case (ON arm) | ∫ Coriolis dt, powered | ∫ centrifugal dt, powered | ∫ cross-heading dt, powered |
|---|---|---|---|
| `gt_baseline` | 147.6 m/s | 12.1 m/s | 84.7 m/s |
| `peg_baseline` | 139.6 m/s | 12.6 m/s | 72.0 m/s |
| `show_cpr` | 140.0 m/s | 12.6 m/s | 70.8 m/s |

Only `gt_baseline`, which has no steering freedom for the swarm to spend, gives a clean number:
**−41.9 kg (0.2 %)**. That is the force-model confound for a passive law and it stands.

What was checked on the `peg_baseline` pair (scripts were session scratch, not kept):

1. **Cross replay.** Propellant is fixed by the decision vector, not by the force model — each
   solution burns the same mass either way. The OFF solution flown with pseudo-forces ON ends
   110 m/s short (h +8.7 km, γ −0.73°) and does not insert; the ON solution flown OFF overshoots by
   153 m/s. Solutions do not transfer between force models.
2. **Local Nelder–Mead refinement** from each solution in each force model: inconclusive. It could
   not recover either arm's own archived optimum (J′ 0.859 against 0.811; 0.959 against 0.819),
   so the `peg_new` objective surface is too rough for it at this scale.
3. **`peg_guidance_new.py` predicts radial acceleration as `−μ/r² + v_θ²/r` only** — no Coriolis
   or Earth-rotation centrifugal term, although its target is the rotating-frame speed. With
   pseudo-forces on the plant carries ~1 m/s² the prediction lacks. This may add to `peg_new`'s
   loss but is not the common cause: `cpr` is open-loop, has no model, and also lost 1.1 t.

**`pso_tail_improvement_frac` ≈ 0 means the swarm stalled, not that it found the global optimum.**
`show_peg` has exactly 0 in both arms and still differs by 7.6 t.

## What it means for Chapter 6

- The confound between `indirect_pmp` (pseudo-force-free) and the other architectures is ~42 kg
  for the gravity turn — small against the ranking gaps.
- For the steered laws it is **unresolved**, not large: at one seed and 100×500 the swarm's choice
  of solution dominates. Do not quote any of the five steered-law differences as physics.
